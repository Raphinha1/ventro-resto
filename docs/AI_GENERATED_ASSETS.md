# AI-generated and uncertain visual assets

No AI-generated graphic is included in this public snapshot. The candidate contained two 508 KB SVG brand files (`logo-ventro.svg` and `logo-ventro-resto.svg`) with embedded raster data. Their Git history only describes a logo/custom visual addition; it does not establish the source image, generation tool, prompt, third-party inputs, or redistribution terms. Their provenance and rights are therefore unresolved, so both files were excluded rather than covered by the project MIT license.

The login, navigation, and public menu use text wordmarks in this snapshot. The KDS references a remotely hosted sound effect; that reference and its playback were removed because its redistribution/source terms were not established. This intentionally leaves the KDS without notification audio.

If the owner later confirms an asset was generated with an AI tool, document that asset separately with its source path, tool/model and date if known, prompt/input provenance, human edits, embedded third-party material, and the applicable distribution terms. Do not imply exclusive copyright or apply MIT to that asset without a separate rights decision.
